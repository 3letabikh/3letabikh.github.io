Method. ALOS 30 m DEM projected to WGS 1984 UTM 36N (EPSG:32636); sea cells removed, sinks filled, D8 flow direction and accumulation; one pour point per river mouth (El Assi and El Hasbani past the border); streams from 0.9 km² of drainage area, Strahler order; morphometry with arcpy and NumPy. Lebanese part of each basin, El Kabir kept whole. Streams on this map: order 3 and above.

Data. ALOS World 3D 30 m (JAXA) · boundaries: OCHA · towns: WorldPop 2025 (most populated cadastres) · basemap: Esri.
